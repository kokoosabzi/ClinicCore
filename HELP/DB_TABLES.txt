# Database Tables Structure

## users
System staff and manager accounts. Important fields: `full_name`, `username`, `password_hash`, `role`, `must_change_password`, timestamps, soft delete.

## patients
Patient profile. Important fields: name, national code, phone, notes, timestamps, soft delete.

## appointments
Appointment lifecycle. Important fields: `patient_id`, `starts_at`, `reason`, `status`, notes, timestamps, soft delete.

## payments
Simple income records. Important fields: `patient_id`, `amount`, `paid_at`, `status`, description, timestamps, soft delete.

## expenses
Simple clinic expense records. Important fields: `title`, `amount`, `spent_at`, `category`, description, timestamps, soft delete.

## messages
Provider-neutral outbound message queue. Important fields: `recipient`, `provider`, `body`, `status`, `error`, timestamps.

## audit_logs
System log table. Important fields: `actor`, `action`, `entity`, `entity_id`, `details`, timestamps.

## Current workflow coverage
The existing tables now support patient search/detail/edit, appointment listing and booking, finance listing, queued messages, and request audit logs without additional migrations.
